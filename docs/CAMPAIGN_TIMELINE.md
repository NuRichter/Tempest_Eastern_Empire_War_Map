# Campaign timeline

Revision R7-CAMPAIGN-RECONSTRUCTION (2026-10-12). The atlas follows the owner's campaign scenario, the Word document *Alur Timeline secara kasarannya* (`Sources of Truth`), phases A to N. Where it and the novels differ, the scenario is authoritative for this atlas and the difference is recorded in the reference register (`DISC-R7-01` to `DISC-R7-13`, shown in the About panel under *Scenario and novels*).

The clock runs from D-43 to D+27 in 10-minute frames. D+0 is first contact (Testarossa's ultimatum and the Main Gate battle). **Every clock time is a simulation placement: the novels give no clock times and no calendar dates.** Day placements are canonical only where the event's `timePrecision` says so. The calendar shown in the interface (year 9001) is an artificial marker.

Provenance: **C** canonical, **CV** canonical with a reconstructed position or time, **S** scenario.

## Dependencies the order respects

- The cohort exists from the start but is drawn inside the main column until the halt. No allocation exists before its split; every split follows its halt or its rejection.
- The eastern cohort's arrival and the halt fall on one frame; so do the 200,000's arrival and the 700,000's.
- Testarossa's group reaches the Main Gate before the 200,000. The ultimatum waits more than two hours. The 40,000 leave only after it is rejected, and the gate holds 240,000 only after they arrive.
- The 60,000 exist from D+7 and die on D+15 (canon); the allied push is engaged before the coup.
- The Third Strike leaves after the coup; both its groups fall before the status quo event.
- The revival follows the Jura dead and precedes the Long Night (canon order).

## How times were chosen

Simulation times sit on the 10-minute grid. They are chosen so that the scenario's simultaneities hold and every march is possible at its unit's stated rate (`world.source.json`, docs/GEOGRAPHIC_MODEL.md §5): the airships at 400 km an hour (v14.txt:1138), the infantry under 120 km a day, armour under 240. The compiler lists any movement over its unit's maximum; R7 has none.

## Validation

`scripts/test-scenario.ts` checks nineteen checkpoints, one per scenario step: A1 to A4, C1 and C2, E1 to E3, F1 to F3, G1, H1, I1, J1, L1, M1 and N1. Each lands on the scenario's figure at its event frame, in the order above, and seeking to a checkpoint gives the same state as replaying up to it.
